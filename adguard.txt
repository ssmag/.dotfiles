! Title: Short-form content blocklist
! Description: Blocks short-form video feeds (YouTube Shorts, Instagram/Facebook
!   Reels, TikTok, Snapchat Spotlight, etc.) while leaving the rest of each site usable.
!
! Usage: AdGuard (browser extension / desktop app) -> Settings -> User rules
!   -> paste this file, or host it and add its raw URL as a custom filter.
! Note: AdGuard Home / AdGuard DNS only understand the domain-wide rules
!   (the "Dedicated short-form apps" section). Path rules ($document) and
!   cosmetic rules (##) need the browser extension or desktop app.

! ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
! YouTube Shorts
! ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Block opening a Short directly (links, bookmarks, typed URLs).
||youtube.com/shorts/$document
! Shorts shelves on home, search, subscriptions and watch-page sidebar.
youtube.com##ytd-reel-shelf-renderer
youtube.com##ytd-rich-shelf-renderer[is-shorts]
youtube.com##ytd-rich-section-renderer:has(ytd-rich-shelf-renderer[is-shorts])
youtube.com##grid-shelf-view-model:has(a[href^="/shorts/"])
youtube.com##ytm-shorts-lockup-view-model
youtube.com##ytm-shorts-lockup-view-model-v2
! Individual Shorts mixed into feeds and search results.
youtube.com##ytd-rich-item-renderer:has(a[href^="/shorts/"])
youtube.com##ytd-video-renderer:has(a[href^="/shorts/"])
youtube.com##ytd-grid-video-renderer:has(a[href^="/shorts/"])
youtube.com##ytd-compact-video-renderer:has(a[href^="/shorts/"])
! "Shorts" entry in the left sidebar and mini sidebar.
youtube.com##ytd-guide-entry-renderer:has(a[title="Shorts"])
youtube.com##ytd-mini-guide-entry-renderer[aria-label="Shorts"]
! "Shorts" tab on channel pages and the search filter chip.
youtube.com##yt-tab-shape[tab-title="Shorts"]
youtube.com##yt-chip-cloud-chip-renderer:has(yt-formatted-string[title="Shorts"])
! Mobile site (m.youtube.com).
m.youtube.com##ytm-reel-shelf-renderer
m.youtube.com##ytm-pivot-bar-item-renderer:has(.pivot-shorts)
m.youtube.com##ytm-video-with-context-renderer:has(a[href^="/shorts/"])
